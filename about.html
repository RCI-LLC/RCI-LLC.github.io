About the Owner

Leonard Wilson, better known as Len, is the founder & owner of Red Cap Industrial LLC. With more than 28 years of hands-on experience in the construction industry. Len has dedicated his career to providing high-quality workmanship & dependable services to bring decades of experience & leadership to every project while creating a company clients can depend on for years to come.

Built to deliver dependable construction work.

Red Cap Industrial LLC has served customers since February 2015, providing expert industrial, commercial, and residential construction across Georgia and South Carolina.

We work across general contracting, project coordination, and site development.
